Turn Hazards into Momentum

In traditional endless runners, collisions end your run. In Bounceback, touching the floor, ceiling, or kinetic bumpers squashes Bouncy-Bot and converts impact force into high-speed rebound combos!

Game Architecture

The "Bounceback" Innovation

Built from the ground up to subvert traditional platformer and runner tropes.

🛡️

Elastic Rebound

Hitting boundaries triggers procedural squash & stretch with immediate upward recoil (-520 px/s) instead of flat grounding.

🔥

5× Bounce Combo

Chaining consecutive rebounds before your combo timer decays stacks multiplier rewards up to 5× on all collected coins and bumpers.

⚡

Kinetic Bumpers

High-tech neon hexagonal bumpers that safely launch Bouncy-Bot away from danger zones while granting +150 bonus score.

🚀

Jet Thruster Physics

Hold Space or Left Click to fire the cyber-orb's downward jet nozzle with dynamic particle trails and pitch tilt interpolation.

Intuitive Controls

One-hand or mouse gameplay designed for fast arcade reflexes.

SPACEBAR Thrust Upward
LEFT CLICK Thrust / Tap
RELEASE Gravity Fall
COLLIDE Auto Bounce
